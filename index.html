Engineering focus — not inventing the candidate
The hard constraint was preventing generated content from inventing skills, qualifications or achievements the user never had. The system runs deterministic rule-based processing as its baseline, validates every output against a schema, and checks each claim back against source evidence in the user's own resume. The Career Mentor is constrained to selecting and ordering verified suggestions rather than writing free-form user-facing text. The product stays fully functional with no AI provider configured at all.
